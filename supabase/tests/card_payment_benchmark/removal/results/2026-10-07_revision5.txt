#     rows, one set-based delete and 500 more decision states — about 40 ms of statement time in a 1.7 s evaluation.
#   * The candidate scan is the evaluator's existing cost of an unresolved leg (one pass over the opposite side of the

#     up to roughly 2,400 legs with distinct amounts; it then compares every pair of legs that have a best candidate:
#         pool 2,000 legs, 2,000 with a best candidate: Nested Loop, Rows Removed by Join Filter: 3998000, 266.6 ms
#         pool 1,500 legs, 1,000 with a best candidate: Nested Loop, Rows Removed by Join Filter:  999000,  70.3 ms
